## Why a journal

After spending the last couple of years building agentic AI systems day-in
and day-out, I realized I never write anything down. Lessons evaporate. Bugs I
spent two days on get re-solved six months later. Cool ideas die in Slack DMs.

So this is the fix: a small, public journal. Short notes. No pressure to be
"polished." Just the raw signal.

## What I'll write about

- **Agentic AI**, multi-agent orchestration, and the weird failure modes
- **RAG, semantic memory** and context engineering tricks
- Things I'm building (PRFlow, side projects, experiments)
- Life stuff — travels, milestones, random reflections

## How this site works

The whole portfolio is a static site, deployed via GitHub Pages. Posts are
just **markdown files in the repo** under `posts/` with a tiny `posts.json`
manifest. There's a private admin page that uses the GitHub API to commit new
posts directly from the browser — no backend, no DB, no CMS.
