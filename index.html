A bit about me

I'm an AI Engineer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. My focus is on the messy, exciting gap between cutting-edge models and reliable production systems — designing multi-agent architectures, retrieval pipelines, and scalable backends that actually hold up under real workloads.

Currently a Software Engineer at InfinitiBit Ltd., where I'm a core contributor to the GraphBit Agentic AI framework and lead engineer on PRFlow, a production multi-agent GitHub PR reviewer.
